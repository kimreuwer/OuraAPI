# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

TwoPandas is a personal-use tool. It is not offered to the public.

## What data is accessed

With the account owner's explicit authorization through Oura's consent screen, TwoPandas reads Oura Ring data from the Oura API V2, including sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, and blood oxygen data, limited to the data types the owner approves.

## Who can see it

Only the account owner and the one person he has authorized. Data is stored locally on that person's computer. It is not uploaded to any server, database, or third-party service operated by TwoPandas.

## What is never done

Data is never sold, shared, published, or used for advertising or any commercial purpose.

## Revoking access

The account owner can revoke access at any time from his Oura account settings or the Oura developer portal. After revocation, no further data can be retrieved. Locally stored copies are deleted on his request.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue in this repository.
